Who is Prime Scale

Two ads specialists. One goal: growth you can measure.

Prime Scale was founded by Reymark Dulutan and D'Lanor Romanes, two social media ads specialists who help businesses turn their marketing efforts into organized, measurable and scalable systems.

We handle campaign strategy, setup, audience targeting, creative coordination, optimization and performance monitoring across Facebook, TikTok, Shopee and other platforms, and we report back with clear numbers so you always know what your budget is doing.

Our goal is simple: combine effective marketing with efficient operations to help businesses generate better results and grow sustainably. Prime Scale · Founders' mission
