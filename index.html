Who is Prime Scale

Prime Scale is a social media ads agency founded by Reymark Dulutan and D'Lanor Romanes. We turn marketing effort into organized, measurable and scalable systems for businesses on Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and Shopee.

We handle campaign strategy, setup, audience targeting, creative coordination, optimization and performance monitoring, then report back with clear numbers so you always know what your budget is doing.
